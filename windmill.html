Windmill Project

A stylized 3D environment built from modular assets — combining art, composition, and real-time performance.

Explore Project

Project Overview

A calm and relaxing sunset aesthetic — a small village nestled deep within the woods, far away from the noise and stress of city life. As dusk settles, the windmill gently winds down, preparing to rest for the peaceful night ahead.

Software Used:

  • Unity 6 HDRP
  • Blender
  • Substance Painter
  • Photoshop
  • TreeIt

Modular Environment Assets

The environment was created using modular assets — a versatile set of nature props elements that can be reused to build cohesive, stylized game environments efficiently.

The trees I first generated in TreeIt and later refined in Blender. Bushes and grass planes were modeled manually in Blender, while rocks were sculpted in Blender and baked in Substance Painter.

Textures for leaves and grass I hand-painted in Photoshop, and the tree bark I sculpted in Blender and baked into a detailed texture in Substance Painter. This pipeline ensured a consistent stylized look across all natural assets.

Every element was made with attention to modularity, visual harmony, and performance within the real-time environment.

The Windmill Model

The windmill was assembled using the modular asset kit, designed to create a cohesive and visually balanced structure for the environment.

This was my first time creating a full modular asset kit — a challenging but rewarding experience. Through research, experimentation, and iteration, I was able to achieve my goal of building a complete stylized windmill.

I modeled the assets in Blender and textured them in Substance Painter. Some materials, such as the brick walls and rock formations, were sculpted in Blender to generate unique normal maps, later refined into tilable textures. This project truly became a “learn-as-you-go” journey that deepened my understanding of modular design and texturing workflows.

Unity Shaders

For this project I also created Custom Shaders. They were created using Unity’s Shader Graph to bring the environment materials to life. These include stylized grass, leaf, rock, and water shaders designed to emphasize surface detail, color variation, and light interaction.

Rock Shader: Uses a triplanar projection technique to eliminate texture stretching and create a cohesive, hand-painted yet dynamic look across the scene.

Water Shader: Built for stylized environments, featuring adjustable parameters for wave strength, depth, and color. It’s flexible enough to adapt to semi-stylized or fully stylized settings — from calm ponds to flowing rivers and open seas.

Tree Shader: Includes adjustable color gradients and wind controls, allowing for subtle leaf movement and natural rustling effects.

Grass Shader: Features customizable color gradients and can also function as a flower shader. A toggle lets users define its mode, while additional controls manage wind gusts and movement intensity for added realism.

Each shader was designed to be flexible, performant, and visually cohesive within the stylized world and also Artist friendly.
