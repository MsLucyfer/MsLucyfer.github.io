Shadow Frames

A psychological horror game set in a deserted 1960s Irish pub — where every photo reveals something hidden within the frame.

Project Overview

Shadow Frames is currently a work in progress, entering its third week of production. The game has atmospheric storytelling, environmental exploration, and subtle psychological horror mechanics inspired by indie titles like I’m on Observation Duty, The Mortuary Assistant and Chilla's art games.

Development Progress

Ideation & First Prototype

To gather interest for the project, I designed and built a small playable prototype to pitch to the students. The process began with brainstorming and wireframing ideas on a MIRO board, followed by developing a functional prototype in Unity to test gameplay flow and atmosphere.
